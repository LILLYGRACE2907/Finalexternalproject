About Facial Emotion Recognition

An end-to-end artificial intelligence web application for real-time facial expression analysis and emotional state classification.

What is Facial Emotion Recognition?

Facial Emotion Recognition (FER) is a key subfield of Computer Vision and Affective Computing. It enables computers to automatically identify human emotional states directly from visual facial expressions.

Facial movements are captured using standard video sensors or webcams, analyzed for key landmarks, normalized, and classified into standard emotional categories using deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

How It Works

  1. Frame Acquisition: The webcam or uploaded photo stream captures video frames at high frame rates.
  2. Face Detection: OpenCV Haar Cascade classifiers detect face coordinates and extract Regions of Interest (ROI).
  3. Image Preprocessing: ROI is converted to 48x48 grayscale, pixel values are normalized to [0,1].
  4. Neural Classification: Deep CNN calculates soft-max probability distributions across 7 emotion classes.

The Seven Primary Emotions

1. Angry

Lowered eyebrows, intense glare, pressed lips signifying frustration or hostility.

2. Disgust

Wrinkled nose, raised upper lip indicating aversion or strong dislike.

3. Fear

Widened eyes, raised eyebrows, open mouth responding to threat cues.

4. Happy

Raised cheek muscles, smiling lips, crinkled eye corners expressing joy.

5. Sad

Drawn-down lip corners, inner eyebrows raised reflecting grief or sorrow.

6. Surprise

High arched eyebrows, wide eyes, dropped jaw reacting to unexpected events.

7. Netural

Relaxed facial muscles without prominent emotional indicators.
